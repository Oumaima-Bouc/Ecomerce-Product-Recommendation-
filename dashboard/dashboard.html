Key Insights & Next Steps 06
Key Findings
Engagement drives recommendations. The engineered engagement_score (clicks + purchases on similar products) had the highest correlation with the target at 0.78 — the strongest predictor overall.
Tree models dominate. XGBoost, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting all exceeded R² = 0.94, far outperforming linear models (R² ≈ 0.88), confirming strong non-linear patterns in the data.
Price matters — but logarithmically. log_price achieved 0.478 correlation vs raw price, showing that price influence on recommendations is better captured after the log transformation.
Clean data = better models. Removing 456 duplicates (31%) and Winsorising 110 outliers reduced noise and directly improved model stability across 5-fold CV.
